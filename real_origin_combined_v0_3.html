PART 1 · CREATOR TRUST

Your creator profile

Build a profile draft now. In the real service, the badge will only say “Human Verified” after an approved provider verifies the creator's identity.

How real verification will work

1Creator requests verification and reviews clear privacy terms.
2An approved provider checks identity securely.
3REAL ORIGIN stores the verification status, not unnecessary identity documents.
4A public profile shows the status and links to a revocable verification record.

Do not enter ID numbers, passwords, or identity documents in this prototype.
